Common questions · Everything you need to know

Straight answers about building, managing, and turning traffic into real customers. No fluff, just results.

❓

How does the process start?

It starts with a zero-pressure discovery conversation. You share your business goals, current traffic situation, and lead targets. We analyze your niche, competitors, and high-intent keywords — then build a tailored roadmap. Within 48h, you’ll receive a clear action plan.

👉 No upfront contracts — just a strategy session to see if we’re a fit.
📞

What happens on the call?

We deep-dive into your lead generation funnel. We’ll review your existing website (if any), identify gaps, and discuss your ICP (ideal customer profile). You’ll learn exactly how we structure high-conversion pages, forms, and CTA placements. It’s collaborative, strategy-heavy, and ends with next steps.

⚙️

Do you only build the site, or manage it too?

Both! We offer full-service management: weekly content updates, lead capture optimization, speed monitoring, security, and performance reports. Or you can choose the “build + handoff” option. Most clients prefer our managed lead generation retainer because that’s where conversion rates climb month over month.

🔍

How do you help with SEO and AI search visibility?

We combine traditional SEO (on-page, technical, backlinks) with next-gen AI visibility: optimizing for Google SGE, ChatGPT referrals, and AI answer engines. Structured data, entity-based content, and conversational FAQ schema. The result? Your brand shows up when buyers ask AI assistants "who builds lead gen sites?"

📈 Clients see +150% organic visibility in AI search results within 3 months.
🚀

How fast can a site go live?

As fast as 5–10 business days for a high-converting lead generation site (landing pages + lead capture + CRM integration). For more complex builds (custom calculators, member areas, etc.), 3-4 weeks. We launch with a staged approach: MVP first → leads rolling → then advanced features.

🔄

Can you improve an existing site, or do you rebuild from scratch?

Absolutely — we love site overhauls. We audit your existing traffic, conversion bottlenecks, and load times. Then we either optimize page-by-page or recommend a strategic rebuild if the foundation is too limited. Many of our clients double their lead flow just by refactoring content and UX.

🐇

What kinds of businesses work with SiteRabbits?

Service providers, local businesses, B2B SaaS, agencies, real estate, home services, consultants, eCommerce leads & more. If you sell expertise or high-ticket offers, we specialize in turning anonymous traffic into qualified leads. Our sweet spot: businesses that want 20+ warm leads per month without wasting ad spend.

💰

How do you turn traffic into leads?

Conversion architecture. We don’t just build pages — we build lead magnets, smart forms, exit-intent popups, chatbots, and automated follow-ups. Combined with behavioral targeting and A/B tested CTAs. Each visitor gets a tailored path: book a call, download a guide, request a quote. Our average client lead conversion rate jumps from 1.5% to 8%+.

🔥 Real example: a contractor went from 6 leads/month → 47 leads/month after our traffic-to-lead system.
